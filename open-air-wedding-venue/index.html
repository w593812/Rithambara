The pool stage

Most open-air venues give you a flat lawn and a stage. At Rithambara the stage is part of the design: a mini pool in front, and a central ramp over the water. The walk to the stage becomes part of the occasion, whether it is the couple, the families or a performance.

Black and white chequered borders frame the pool, lantern-lit paths line the lawn, and palms and open views surround the seating. The lawn is fully customisable, so your decorator can set it to the theme you choose.

Best for

  • Engagements, sangeet and mehendi evenings

Questions about the open-air venue

How many guests can the open-air venue hold?

It seats 250 guests, with dining for 150, across 11,500 sq. ft. of lawn.

What is the pool stage?

The stage sits in front of a mini pool, and a ramp runs through the centre of the water to reach it. Couples, families and performers walk across the water to the stage.

Can I decorate the lawn to my own theme?

Yes. The open-air venue is fully customisable to your preferred theme.

Are there bridal rooms and parking?

Yes. There are two air-conditioned bridal rooms at this venue, plus 14,000 sq. ft. of parking and full power backup.

What are the slot timings?

Morning is 6 AM to 3 PM and evening is 3 PM to 11 PM. Full-day bookings are available.

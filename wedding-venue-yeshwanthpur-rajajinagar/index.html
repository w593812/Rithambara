Why couples from Yeshwanthpur & Rajajinagar choose Rithambara

A drive out of the busy neighbourhoods buys a calm, open setting with room to breathe. Two air-conditioned bridal rooms at each venue let the families rest during a long wedding day.

  • Open-air lawn for 250 guests with a pool-front stage and a ramp over the water

  • 14,000 sq. ft. of parking, two A/C bridal rooms per venue, 100% power backup
  • In-house catering or your own caterer, and morning or evening slots

Is Rithambara easy to reach from Yeshwanthpur?

Rithambara is on Hesarghatta Road, Hurulichikanahalli, Bengaluru 560090. Use the live directions button on this page for the drive time from your area at your event hour. Weekend evenings are slower.

How many guests can Rithambara hold?

The open-air venue seats 250 guests and the banquet hall seats 300, with dining for 150 and 180 respectively.

Can we bring our own caterer?

Yes. In-house catering is available, and you can also bring your preferred caterer.

Is there parking for a large guest list?

Yes. There is 14,000 sq. ft. of dedicated parking and full power backup.

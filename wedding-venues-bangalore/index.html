Questions

Where is Rithambara in Bangalore?

Rithambara is on Hesarghatta Road, Hurulichikanahalli, in North Bengaluru (560090).

Is Rithambara good for guests from across Bangalore?

Yes, if you want a destination venue with a lot of space and parking. Guests from the south and east of the city should check the live drive time for the event hour.

What makes Rithambara different?

Its open-air venue has a stage in front of a pool, with a ramp across the water. It also has an indoor banquet hall on the same campus.

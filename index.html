Questions we hear most

How many guests can Rithambara hold?

Venue 1 seats 250 guests with dining for 150. Venue 2 seats 300 guests with dining for 180.

What are the slot timings?

Morning runs 6 AM to 3 PM and evening runs 3 PM to 11 PM. You can also book the full day.

Can I bring my own caterer?

Yes. We offer in-house catering, and you are welcome to bring an external caterer.

Is there parking and power backup?

There is 14,000 sq. ft. of parking and 100% generator backup for lighting and sound.

Does Rithambara have a stage with a pool?

Yes. Venue 1 is an open-air lawn with a stage set in front of a mini pool, and a ramp runs through the centre of the water to the stage.

Where is the venue?

On Hesarghatta Road, Hurulichikanahalli, Bengaluru 560090, in North Bengaluru.

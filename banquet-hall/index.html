An indoor hall with character

The banquet hall is built around a high pitched timber ceiling, with grand chandeliers over the seating and slate underfoot. A brick emblem feature wall, a stage with golden seating and a VIP sofa lounge give you ready-made backdrops for photographs.

It works for weddings, receptions, engagements and larger family functions, and it pairs well with the open-air lawn if you want ceremony and dinner in two settings.

Questions about the banquet hall

How many guests does the banquet hall hold?

It seats 300 guests, with dining for 180, across 14,000 sq. ft.

What does the hall look like?

It has high pitched timber ceilings, crystal chandeliers, slate flooring, a terracotta brick entrance and a brick feature wall.

Is it air-conditioned with bridal rooms?

The venue has two air-conditioned bridal rooms. Ask our team about cooling for the main hall when you enquire.

Can we use our own caterer?

Yes. You can use our in-house catering or bring your preferred external caterer.

Is there parking and power backup?

Yes. There is 14,000 sq. ft. of parking and 100% power backup for lighting and sound.

Want the open-air pool stage too?

Both venues are on one campus, so you can plan a ceremony outdoors and dinner indoors.
